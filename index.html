Sizing and warehouse QC checklist

Before ordering

  • Save the seller URL, variant, size and price with the date you checked it.
  • For shoes, compare foot length with the listing’s sizing method. For clothing, measure a garment you already wear and compare it with the seller’s chart.

Before approving shipment

  • Match warehouse photos to your order and check the size label, color and quantity.
  • Ask for measurements or close-ups of unclear details, such as a zipper, sole or seam. Check the return window before approving the parcel.

Estimate your parcel budget

Check the destination and packed dimensions when comparing shipping quotes. Enter each amount in USD and include fees only where they are charged separately.

LoveGoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product directory for comparing finds and tracking seller links before placing an order through LoveGoBuy. This page features a selection of real CAFANS listings.

Do the item prices include shipping?

The displayed prices cover the listed items. Check domestic delivery, international freight, service fees and any destination charges separately.

Where do I find QC photos for my item?

Check your order in the shopping agent’s warehouse view when photos become available. The images in this catalog show the listings. Use your order’s QC photos to check the specific item being shipped.
